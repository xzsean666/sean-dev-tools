Privacy Policy

App Name: Sean Dev Tools · Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Sean Dev Tools accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the application or authenticate via Google OAuth.

2. Information We Access

Depending on the scopes you explicitly approve during sign-in, Sean Dev Tools may access:

  • Basic account identifiers (such as your email address and display name) needed to authenticate your session.
  • Read or operational data strictly within the API scopes you authorize (for example, analytics or tool-specific data requested by you).

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google APIs is used solely to provide and maintain the features you explicitly request in Sean Dev Tools. We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with unauthorized third parties.

Sean Dev Tools's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage & Retention

OAuth tokens and necessary configuration data are stored only as long as required to provide the requested functionality. You can revoke Sean Dev Tools's access at any time from your Google Account Permissions page.

5. Contact & Data Deletion

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request deletion of any stored data, please open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the developer using the support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
