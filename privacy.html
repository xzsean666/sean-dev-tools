Privacy Policy

Application Name: Sean Dev Tools · Effective Date: November 25, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Sean Dev Tools (“the Application”, “we”, “our”) collects, accesses, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website (https://xzsean666.github.io/sean-dev-tools/) or authorize the Application via Google OAuth 2.0.

2. Information We Collect and Access

We only request access to the minimum data necessary to operate the features you explicitly initiate. Depending on the OAuth scopes you approve, Sean Dev Tools may access:

  • Account Identity Data: Basic Google profile details such as your email address and account identifier used solely to identify your authorized session.
  • Authorized Service & Analytics Data: Read-only metrics, reports, or resource metadata within the specific Google API scopes you consent to (for example, analytics reporting or property diagnostics).
  • OAuth Credentials: Access tokens and refresh tokens issued by Google solely to execute authorized API requests on your behalf.

Static pages hosted on GitHub Pages do not set advertising cookies or run third-party behavioral tracking scripts.

3. How We Use Information

All data accessed through Sean Dev Tools is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Authenticating your authorized session and verifying access to resources you own or manage.
  • Retrieving and displaying requested analytics metrics, operational summaries, and developer diagnostics.
  • Maintaining, debugging, and securing the reliability of the Application.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or transfer your Google user data to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks, and we do not use Google user data to train AI/ML models or generate prohibited content.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Sean Dev Tools’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • Data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features prominent in the Application’s user interface.
  • Data is never transferred to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve user-facing features, comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.
  • Humans do not read user data unless you give affirmative consent for specific troubleshooting, it is necessary for security purposes, or it is required to comply with law.

5. Data Storage, Security & Retention

OAuth tokens and retrieved operational outputs are stored in protected environments controlled by the account owner and protected using standard access controls and HTTPS transport encryption.

We retain OAuth tokens and cached data only for as long as needed to provide the active integration. When you disconnect the Application or revoke permissions, associated tokens become invalid and any locally cached session data is deleted upon request or routine cleanup.

6. Your Rights, Access Revocation & Data Deletion

You remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • Revoke Access: Remove Sean Dev Tools from your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • Request Deletion: Submit a deletion inquiry via our GitHub Issue Tracker or contact the developer through the support email displayed on the OAuth consent screen. Requests are processed within 7 business days.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect feature updates or legal requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
